Pain point

Can't find features in the new menu

10unique people

People can't locate features they know exist (Meetings, Meeting Rooms, Creator Studio, Ticketing, Service calendars, Settings, Media Library) after the redesign. Some are rollout or Labs questions, but most say the new menu hides where things went.

What we could do: Add 'where did it go' redirects or tooltips for moved items, and surface Labs and beta features in a predictable place in the menu.

Feature request

Sidebar colors, branding and custom CSS

9unique people

Agencies want to recolor the sidebar, apply brand colors in sub-accounts, and keep or rebuild their custom CSS/JS on the new layout. Several are already shipping their own CSS themes.

What we could do: Ship sidebar theme tokens (background, text, active and hover colors) at agency and sub-account level, and document stable CSS hooks for the new nav.

Pain point

More clicks and nested flyouts

4unique people

Multiple drop-downs, flyouts and sub-navigation make common destinations feel two or more clicks further away, and people have to relearn the layout.

What we could do: Audit the click depth of the top 20 destinations; let people pin deep items to L1 and keep the last-used sub-page per section.
