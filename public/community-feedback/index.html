How this is put together

  • Source: the HighLevel – Official Community Facebook group, posts since Oct 26, 2026, when the new navigation was released. Each post appears once, even if several searches found it.
  • Posts are found by keyword searches (menu, sidebar, navigation, new UI, customize, can't find, and others) and a day-by-day sweep of the group feed.
  • "People" means unique names: post authors plus commenters who echo or +1 the same point. HighLevel staff are never counted.
  • The page refreshes automatically at 11:00 AM and 4:30 PM IST. It contains customer names and quotes, so share it only internally.
