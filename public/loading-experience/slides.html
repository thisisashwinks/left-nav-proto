2 · The recommendation

6 principles for every wait

1 · Ownership

The shell owns the first loader

The host document paints the shell and a route-shaped skeleton before any module JavaScript.

2 · One per region

One indicator per region, start to finish

The skeleton hands off to content with a cross-fade. Never skeleton → spinner.

3 · Time ladder

Wait, then escalate on a fixed ladder

Nothing under 300 ms → indicator → words at 2 s → “Still working on it” at 8 s → failure at 30 s.

4 · Exit

Every loader has an exit

A failure state in place, in plain copy, with Try again and Go back.

5 · Continuity

Keep what's on screen

Refresh, filter, sort, and paginate keep stale content. Never re-skeleton loaded content.

6 · White-label

Neutral and themeable

Every loader reads 1 color token, neutral skeletons, no logos, plain-verb copy. Ready for agency colors later.

  • These 6 are what we propose to adopt as the HighRise loading standard for every team.
  • 1 and 2 remove the chain of loaders. 3 and 4 make every wait predictable and finite.
  • 5 protects work already on screen. 6 keeps us safe for agencies.

Route change

Route change, step by step

  1. 0 msBreadcrumb and nav switch to the destination
  2. 0–300 msNothing else changes. Fast pages never show a loader.
  3. 300 msProgress line appears and trickles toward 90%
  4. 300 msOld page dims and goes inert; a stuck loader is swapped for the destination skeleton
  5. 300 msModule not mounted? The shell shows the destination skeleton; the module takes it over
  6. 2 s“Loading {page}…” in the breadcrumb slot
  7. 8 s“Still working on it”
  8. 30 s“Couldn't load {page}” with Try again and Go back
  9. DoneContent cross-fades in 200 ms; the line completes and fades

Principle 4

Every loader has an exit

HangThe API never answers

At 30 s: “Couldn't load {page}” · Check your connection and try again. Time runs 4× here.

Partial1 widget fails

“Some of this page didn't load” · 3 of 4 widgets loaded. The failed card fails in place.

  • Failure lives in the canvas, in place, never as a toast echo. 1 message only.
  • Try again only appears when the request is retryable. Go back always works.
  • Focus moves to the failure title when the user started the action.
  • QA hang test: block the API, every loader must reach content or failure by 30 s.
Principle 5

Keep what's on screen

  • Refresh, filter, sort, and paginate keep the rows at 100%
  • A 2 px edge line on the region shows work is happening
  • “Updated 3:04 PM” says what they're looking at
  • Refresh becomes Cancel after 2 s
  • Never re-skeleton loaded content
Proposed · table refresh
  • Today, a filter change can wipe a loaded table back to a skeleton. That reads as “my data is gone”.
  • Stale rows stay readable and clickable; the edge line is enough signal.
  • Same rule for cached views: “Showing your last {thing} · Checking for new ones”.

Principle 6

White-label and accessibility

Every loader reads 1 color token

Accessibility
  • aria-busy="true" on every loading region
  • 1 always-present role="status" live region
  • Screen readers announce each page change twice, no more: “Loading Contacts” when it starts, “Contacts loaded” when it’s ready
  • Pending buttons keep focus with aria-disabled
  • Focus moves to the failure title when the user started the action
  • Reduced motion: no translate, no glow; spinners keep rotating
  • Spinners, the line, and bars use --primary-600 or currentColor. Nothing else is colored.
  • Agency brand colors aren't supported yet. When they are, loaders follow automatically, with no rework (decision 5).
  • No logos, marks, sparkles, or mascots as loaders. No vendor names in loading copy.
Copy

Name the work, in plain words

Do say

  • Loading your account
  • Loading {page}…
  • Still working on it
  • Switching to {name}
  • Showing your last {thing} · Checking for new ones
  • You're offline. We'll retry when you're back.
  • Couldn't load {page} · Check your connection and try again.
  • Importing contacts · 1,240 of 3,100

Never say

  • Please waitSay what's happening: “Loading Contacts…”
  • Loading… (alone, after 2 s)Name the page or the work.
  • Error 503Plain cause and an action. Codes go behind Details.
  • Don't close this windowMove the work to the background jobs panel.
  • Something went wrongNever without Try again or Go back.
  • Staging already has most of the right strings. The failure strings exist but never show today.
  • Every string is localized; {page} is the user-facing name from the nav.
  • Past tense on success: “Saving…” → “Saved”.

For review

5 decisions we need today

1

Remove the full-window boot bar?

Yes. Keep 1 line, under the header, for route changes only.

2

Is 30 s the right timeout?

Approve 30 s as the default. Per-region override with a review; calibrate against P95 after launch.

3

Who builds which loaders?

Platform Frontend and Switchyard, together: the shared loaders (app shell, progress line, status words, timeouts, failure states). Product teams: the skeletons for their own pages.

4

Keep 2 “slow” thresholds?

Keep both. Boot at 10 s can't run JavaScript; everything else at 8 s.

5

Should loaders be ready for agency brand colors?

Yes. Every loader reads 1 color token: HighLevel blue today. When agency colors are supported, loaders follow automatically. Skeletons stay neutral gray.

  • Each has a recommendation; we're asking for a yes or a named concern.
  • Decisions 1, 2, and 5 unblock the page-level work today.
  • On 2: 30 s matches the cap already in the shipped shell, so nobody waits longer than today.
